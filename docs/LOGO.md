# TuxCue logo

The app mark is a custom AI-generated penguin wearing lime headphones, with a play triangle in its belly. The generated PNG has a transparent background and is used in the interface, browser icon and AppImage desktop icon.

Asset: `frontend/public/tuxcue-logo.png`. Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, not the API/CLI fallback. The full-size original is retained unchanged.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Create one finished square app logo mark for TuxCue, a Linux soundboard for triggering audio clips in voice chat. Icon only, no text. A distinctive, simple geometric penguin wearing headphones, with one unmistakable right-pointing play triangle in its white belly. Compact unified silhouette, friendly but polished, strong negative space, few bold shapes, readable at small desktop app-icon sizes. Flat vector-like design with crisp edges. Match the existing app palette: deep charcoal #131719, warm white #e8edee, bright soft lime #c4ed83; use lime for the headphones or play triangle as the single accent. Keep the penguin recognisable and visually balanced; no tiny decorative details, no sound waves, no gradients, no 3D, no shadows, no extra objects, no letters, no wordmark, no watermark. Truly transparent background with preserved alpha, no background square and no checkerboard pattern. Centre the single mark and have it fill approximately 85 percent of a 1024 by 1024 canvas. This is the actual app logo, not a mockup or presentation board.

The source PNG retains its AI-generation provenance metadata. It is not presented as the official Linux/Tux artwork or as a copy of Larry Ewing’s Tux. This description makes no guarantee of exclusive copyright protection. See [TRADEMARKS.md](../TRADEMARKS.md) for project identity guidance.
